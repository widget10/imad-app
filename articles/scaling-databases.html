Scaling Databases

Challenges in database scaling: As applications grow and user bases expand, databases often become bottlenecks. Scaling databases presents unique challenges due to the need to maintain data consistency, integrity, and availability.

Vertical vs. Horizontal scaling: Vertical scaling (scaling up) involves increasing the resources (CPU, RAM, storage) of a single server. Horizontal scaling (scaling out) involves distributing the database load across multiple servers. Each approach has its trade-offs.

Common techniques for scaling databases include sharding (partitioning data across multiple databases), replication (creating copies of the database for read-heavy workloads), caching, and connection pooling. Choosing the right techniques depends on the specific requirements of the application.
